info Purpose of Medication
expand_more

Lisinopril is used to treat high blood pressure (hypertension). Lowering high blood pressure helps prevent strokes, heart attacks, and kidney problems. It is also used to treat heart failure and to improve survival after a heart attack. It works by relaxing blood vessels so blood can flow more easily.

report_problem Potential Side Effects
expand_more

  • Rare but Serious: Fainting, symptoms of high potassium (muscle weakness, slow heart rate), or signs of kidney problems.
